About Zdorovie

Care that grows with you

Everyone ages — it's an inherent part of life. Zdorovie's trained staff work closely with participants and families to understand each person's needs and build a plan that helps them keep living the life they want.

Our preventative, hands-on approach is designed to protect the health and independence of the elders we serve.
